How it works

From the label to a cited answer.

  1. 01

    Read

    Point the camera at an ingredient list or a barcode. An AI reader transcribes it, and on-device OCR takes over when there's no signal or no key.

  2. 02

    Match

    Every name goes through the canon: one entry per ingredient, with every name it goes by, in several languages.

    415 ingredients · 1,743 names

  3. 03

    Check

    Each ingredient is looked up in six official lists, mirrored daily, and in curated findings. Every row carries a citation.

    6,557 entries mirrored

  4. 04

    Explain

    Every sentence comes from a fixed template with its source. A model never decides what differs between two labels.

The record

Who decided, with the source.

Bans, pending bans, warning labels and hazard listings, by jurisdiction. Each finding names the regulation behind it and links to it, with the date the list was last checked.

  • Banned · EU

    Potassium Bromate is not permitted in food in the European Union.

    Regulation (EC) No 1333/2008 on food additives, Annex II Union list

  • Banned · India

    Potassium Bromate is not permitted in food in India.

    FSSAI removal of potassium bromate from the permitted additives list · in force since 20 June 2016

  • Pending ban · California

    California has adopted a ban on Potassium Bromate taking effect 1 January 2027. It remains permitted until then.

    California Food Safety Act (AB 418), Health & Safety Code s.109025

Compare

Same brand. Two countries.

Line up one product's labels from two markets and see exactly what differs. Lay's in India listed palmolein where the US label listed sunflower, corn or canola oil.

“Verso shows what the two labels say. It doesn't say why they differ.”

From the comparison screen

Differences are computed in code, after both lists go through the canon. Fat ranges come from the Codex standard for named vegetable oils. Shown on Verso test labels.

The score

Honest about what it knows.

A 0–100 score where points come off only for bans, warnings and hazard listings on record. It is withheld when too little of the label has been checked, so an additive nobody has researched never reads as fine.

Open source

Run it yourself.

Code under MIT, curated data under CC BY-NC 4.0. Label photos, and the names of ingredients Verso hasn't seen before, go only to the AI provider you configure. With none configured, labels are read on the device.

Quick start · Node 22.13+, pnpm, Docker

pnpm install
cp .env.example .env
pnpm db:up && pnpm migrate && pnpm seed
pnpm sources:sync
pnpm api
pnpm --filter @verso/mobile web

Development builds show test labels on the Scan tab. They run the real API and data and skip only the camera.

Verso

Verso does not produce the regulatory or hazard information it shows. Each item is published by the official source linked beside it, a regulator, a WHO agency or a court, and is dated by when that source was last checked. Verso can't guarantee it. It is not medical, nutritional or legal advice.

Verso is the reverse side of a printed page: the ingredient panel is on the back of the pack.
