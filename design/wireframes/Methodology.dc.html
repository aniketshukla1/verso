How scores work
Two numbers, never merged. Additive hazard and nutrition rest on different evidence, and averaging them is how scoring apps lose credibility.
WHAT COUNTS, AND HOW MUCH
A
Regulatory status Banned, restricted or warning-required in a named place
Drives the score
B
Body classification IARC group, ADI exceeded, Prop 65 listing
Strong input
C
Peer-reviewed research Graded by study design and replication
Context only
D
Contested or emerging Single studies, animal-only at implausible doses
Never counts
When we show no score at all If we could not read most of the label, or we have researched too few of its ingredients, you get the findings and no number. A number would claim we had checked things we have not.
WHAT WE WILL NEVER SAY That an ingredient causes a disease. We report what a regulator did and what a body classified, with the date and the instrument. You draw the conclusion.
